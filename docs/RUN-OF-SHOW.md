# Run of show

There are two game lengths, picked with **G** on the dashboard's title screen:

- **Short game (4 years):** the original design, for one 50-minute period. The beat sheet
  directly below.
- **Full game (7 years):** adds The Trail Network, Where the Profit Goes, and The Mill or the
  Carbon. About 75 minutes, for a double block. See [Full game](#full-game-7-years-about-75-minutes)
  at the end.

## Short game (4 years, 50 minutes)

A beat sheet for the 50-minute period. Budget roughly 42 minutes of live content
after students settle and before they file out. Times are targets, not a straitjacket.
The one rule if you run short: **protect Round 3.** It is the richest round and the one
the whole ethics arc turns on. Compress Round 2 or the epilogue before you touch it.

All meter changes referenced here are defined in `SCENARIOS.md`. The facilitator drives
every dashboard change with a key press (see `DASHBOARD-SPEC.md`).

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 0:00-0:03 | **Cold open** | No introduction. Ask: "Above the town of Cedar Bend is Thunderhead Mountain. There is timber on its lower slopes, right above people's homes. I am going to make you decide whether we cut it. But first: who owns it?" Let them answer. Most will be wrong, and being wrong out loud is engaging. Only after they have guessed do you give your one-line introduction. |
| 0:03-0:06 | **Welcome and setup** | Welcome screen: the class has been selected as the corporate board of the Thunderhead Mountain Community Forest, owned by the Town of Cedar Bend; then, in a minute, what a community forest is. Then the setup screen. Explain the four meters in under two minutes (if it takes longer, the dashboard is too complicated, fix the dashboard not the explanation). Assign the three room blocs and the voiceless seat (see `CLASSROOM-MECHANICS.md`). State the one rule: if the treasury hits zero, the Province takes the forest back, game over. |
| 0:06-0:13 | **Round 1: The First Harvest** | The forest has to fund itself. How hard do you cut this year? Bloc caucus, floor debate, private ballot, public vote. Apply result live. Name the carry-forward consequence out loud. |
| 0:13-0:21 | **Round 2: The Windfall** | A salvage-logging opportunity or an outside buyout offer. Tests whether they trade the long game for fast money, and introduces "who captures the value" when an outsider wants in. |
| 0:21-0:31 | **Round 3: The Partnership** | The Ktunaxa and/or Secwépemc propose co-management. This is a real decision with real tradeoffs, not a footnote. It reframes the word "community": whose forest is it? Give it the most air. |
| 0:31-0:38 | **Round 4: Fire Season** | Their accumulated Forest Health / Wildfire Risk determines their exposure. The boards that preserved everything now face the highest fuel load above the homes. The past comes home and the meter does the moralizing. |
| 0:38-0:43 | **Epilogue and the reveal** | Read the "50 years later" outcome off their final meter state. Then show the opening vote next to the closing vote and let them see that the room changed its own mind. |
| 0:43-0:50 | **Debrief and close** | Name the business concepts they already used, then the reveal that this is not made up (Golden's 2006 community forest allocation, lost in 2010, being tried again today) (do not lecture them first, cash them in after): profit vs break-even, who captures value, renewable vs non-renewable, social entrepreneurship. Then the 80-year close. Leave time for two or three real questions. |

## Facilitator notes

- **Keep the concept-naming short.** The moment the debrief feels like the lesson has
  "arrived," you lose them. Land each concept in a sentence and move.
- **Every reveal is a chance to move the meters visibly.** The reaction of the room to
  the dashboard reacting is a good chunk of the energy. Do not rush the apply-and-animate
  moment; let them watch the bar move.
- **If a bloc goes quiet**, call on it by its interest, not by name: "Recreation, this cut
  runs through your trails, are you really going to let that pass?"
- **Fast votes early, slower votes later.** Round 1 can move quickly. Rounds 3 and 4
  deserve the debate. Do not spend your debate budget on the easy round.

## Full game (7 years, about 75 minutes)

The same arc with three extra years. The new years are quicker decisions (hands only, no
ballot, about 4 minutes each), so the debate budget still goes to The Partnership and Fire
Season. Budget about 67 minutes of live content.

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 0:00-0:03 | **Cold open** | As in the short game. Take the opening vote. |
| 0:03-0:07 | **Setup** | As in the short game. |
| 0:07-0:14 | **Year 1: The First Harvest** | Ballot, then the Residents knot and a re-vote (U). |
| 0:14-0:21 | **Year 2: The Windfall** | The outside buyout. It will come back to bite them twice. |
| 0:21-0:26 | **Year 3: The Trail Network** | *Quick round.* Charge for access (a paid shuttle and parking), build with the trail groups, or keep it a working forest. The pricing round: a forest sells more than timber. Optional 4 Ps drop-in here. |
| 0:26-0:37 | **Year 4: The Partnership** | Protect this. Most air of the session. |
| 0:37-0:42 | **Year 5: Where the Profit Goes** | *Quick round.* A good timber year leaves a surplus: community dividend, a reserve fund, or a FireSmart crew. Social enterprise in one question: who is the profit for? (The reserve quietly pays back in fire season.) |
| 0:42-0:49 | **Year 6: The Mill or the Carbon** | The mill needs a log contract; a carbon buyer pays the same to leave the trees standing. Carbon credits keep the forest dense above the homes and make thinning in Year 7 expensive. Let the Residents discover that. |
| 0:49-0:57 | **Year 7: Fire Season** | As in the short game, with seven years of choices behind the slider. |
| 0:57-1:03 | **Epilogue and the reveal** | Outcome, then opening vote against closing vote. |
| 1:03-1:15 | **Debrief and close** | Seven concepts to cash in (adds pricing/revenue streams and where the profit goes). Then the 80-year close. |

If you are running long, the first round to cut to a 2-minute hands vote is Where the Profit
Goes, then The Trail Network. Never compress The Partnership.
